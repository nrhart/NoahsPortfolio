Decision Making

A browser build of my original openFrameworks C++ project. Explore decision trees, behavior trees, and goal-oriented action planning (GOAP).

Choose a scene, then use Start / Reset. Click to set a target in the Decision Tree and Behavior Tree scenes, or relocate bait in GOAP. Wall building pauses the simulation and lets you paint obstacles in the first two scenes; GOAP uses its own environment. Fullscreen makes the original debug information easier to read.
